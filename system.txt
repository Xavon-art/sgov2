0. ONE-MINUTE OVERVIEW
----------------------
SmartGov is a federated student-government portal:

  - One USG (University Student Government) owns the organization.
  - Many LSGs (Local Student Governments / colleges) belong to it.
  - USG reviews, approves, monitors, and publishes organization-wide data.
  - LSG submits requests, tasks, events, reports, budgets, documents, and structure charts for its own unit only.
  - Public users can answer surveys and feedback forms through anonymous links.

Core rules:

  1. Session-based auth. Cookie: sgov_session, httpOnly, SameSite=Lax, 30 days.
  2. Identity is resolved on the server by currentAccount() -> smartgov.resolve_account.
  3. The browser never decides final authority. RPCs in Postgres enforce auth, org, and unit scope.
  4. Tables are RLS-protected with no broad direct policies. Authoritative writes go through RPCs.
  5. Statuses are shared vocabulary; UI labels are mapped in src/lib/*.ts.
  6. Files are catalog metadata only in lists. Bytes are downloaded through scoped routes/RPCs.
  7. Time-based state (expired requests, closed surveys, pauses) is computed lazily on read.
  8. If SMTP is absent, mail goes to smartgov.outbox and can be viewed at /dev/outbox in dev.
